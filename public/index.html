Compliant with Information Technology Act, 2000 & DPDP Framework

1. Data Collection Architecture

We process only requisite Personally Identifiable Information (PII) indispensable for verifying identity, facilitating order fulfillment, and complying with statutory tax obligations:

  • Identity & Communication Data: Full customer name, verified email address, and mobile phone number.
  • Logistics & Consignment Data: Complete delivery address, landmark, PIN code, and recipient designation.
  • Transaction Identifiers: Razorpay Payment ID, Order ID, and cryptographic authentication tokens.
  • Security & Network Telemetry: Anonymized IP addresses, browser client metadata, and timestamped security audit logs.

2. Purpose & Principles of Data Utilization

Collected customer records are deployed solely for order processing, logistics consignment booking, real-time shipment dispatch notification (via WhatsApp/Email), and account management. We do not sell, lease, trade, or monetize customer data with third-party advertising brokers under any circumstances.

3. Banking & Payment Token Security

Our infrastructure does not store complete credit/debit card credentials, CVV numbers, or banking PINs. All financial settlement operations are handled directly through Reserve Bank of India (RBI) authorized payment gateways.

4. Customer Security & Password Encryption

Customer account credentials are safeguarded using one-way adaptive cryptographic hashing (bcrypt with multi-round salt). Customers maintain full autonomy to request profile rectification or permanent data deletion by contacting support.

Transparency in Storage & Tracking Technologies

1. Function and Scope of Cookies

Cookies and browser local storage technologies are compact data tokens placed on your device to maintain session integrity, optimize website speed, and deliver a coherent user experience.

2. Categorization of Utilized Cookies

  • Strictly Essential Functional Storage: Manages persistent shopping cart items, maintains secure customer login tokens (JSON Web Tokens), and preserves checkout state during navigation.
  • Preference Tokens: Retains user visual themes (Dark Mode / Light Mode) and preferred sorting criteria.
  • Infrastructure & Rate-Limiting Protection: Monitors client request frequencies to protect the web server against automated DDoS attacks, brute-force vulnerabilities, and bot crawling.

3. User Consent & Management

Users may configure browser settings to decline or clear cookies at will. Note that disabling essential session storage may impair shopping cart retention and account authentication.

Pan-India Express Logistics & Delivery Standards

1. Packaging Integrity & Dispatch Schedule

Art supplies, liquid resins, pigments, and botanical specimens undergo meticulous quality inspection and shock-resistant multi-layered bubble packaging to ensure safe transit. All verified orders are dispatched within 24 to 48 business hours from our Surendranagar studio.

2. Estimated Transit Timelines across India

  • Gujarat & Western Region: 2 to 4 Business Days.
  • Metro Cities & Major Urban Hubs: 3 to 6 Business Days.
  • Tier 2 / Tier 3 Cities & Regional Centers: 4 to 7 Business Days.
  • Special Transit Zones (Northeast / Remote Locations): 6 to 9 Business Days.

3. Courier Consignment Tracking

Upon consignment generation, an automated electronic dispatch notification containing the assigned Air Waybill (AWB) number and direct live tracking hyperlink is transmitted via WhatsApp and Email.

4. Shipping Tariffs & Free Delivery Thresholds

Standard flat-rate shipping is computed based on gross volumetric package weight and destination pincode. RK Resin Art periodically sponsors Free Shipping on qualifying orders exceeding established purchase thresholds.

Customer Protection, Transit Guarantee & Replacement Protocol

1. Transit Damage Guarantee

We take complete responsibility for consignments during transit. If an item arrives physically damaged, leaking, or defective, RK Resin Art guarantees an expedited free replacement or full monetary refund.

Mandatory Claim Requirement: To prevent fraudulent claims and initiate immediate courier insurance compensation, a continuous, uncut 360° unboxing video showing the outer shipping label and intact parcel seal opening is mandatory for all transit damage, leakage, or missing item claims.

2. Incident Notification Window

Transit discrepancies or product defects must be formally reported within 48 hours of courier delivered timestamp via WhatsApp at +91 81419 94995 or email at rinkupatel3495@gmail.com accompanied by your Order ID and unboxing footage.

3. Settlement & Refund Disbursement Timeline

Upon verification by our support team, replacement articles are dispatched within 24 hours. Approved monetary refunds are processed directly to the customer's original payment source / UPI account within 3 to 5 business days.
